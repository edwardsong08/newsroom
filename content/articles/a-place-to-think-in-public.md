# A place to think in public

> Why this corner of the internet exists, and what I hope to leave here over time.

The best work usually leaves a trail: a decision that was harder than it looked,
a small idea that changed the shape of a project, or a question worth carrying
into the next one. This blog is where I plan to keep that trail.

I want it to be useful without pretending to be definitive. Expect notes from
things I am building, lessons that arrived a little late, and occasional detours
into whatever has my attention.

## What belongs here

Mostly: software, systems, design, and the craft of making useful things. But a
workbench is interesting because unrelated tools end up beside one another, so I
am leaving the edges deliberately open.

The plan is simple—notice carefully, explain clearly, and publish when there is
something worth sharing. Welcome in.
